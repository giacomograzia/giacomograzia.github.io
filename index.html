About

I'm an MSc student in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Amsterdam, with a BSc in Data Science from TU Eindhoven and Tilburg University. My work so far spans medical image segmentation and fairness and privacy in large language models.

I like rigorous empirical research: reproducing methods carefully, evaluating them systematically on large datasets, and being honest about what the numbers show. I run most of my experiments on the Snellius HPC cluster and a self-managed remote server.

Publications

Mixture of Experts for Fine-Grained Shape Refinement in Medical Segmentation

MICCAI Shape in Medical Imaging Workshop, 2026

A mixture-of-experts refiner for small pelvic-fracture fragments in X-rays, evaluated on 26,000+ fragments. It reduces boundary error by over 65% versus a strong baseline.

Paper

Reproducing “The Tug of War Within: Mitigating the Fairness–Privacy Conflicts in Large Language Models”

Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR), 2026

Reproduces and extends SPIN across three instruction-tuned 7B LLMs: up to 6.1 percentage points better fairness–privacy trade-offs with at most a 0.6-point drop in general capability, plus new experiments on malicious data, limited data and safety–helpfulness conflicts.

Information extraction from Dutch administrative decisions

BSc thesis · CITaDOG project, Tilburg University

Extracting recipients, dates, legal references and legal effects from decisions by the Dutch competition authority (ACM), comparing rule-based, NER and LLM approaches. Up to 97% on key information and 90% on legal-outcome identification.

Sentiment classification on the Stanford Sentiment Treebank

Coursework · University of Amsterdam · with Denisa Checiu

Six architectures from bag-of-words to Tree-LSTMs on 5-class SST, isolating the effects of word order, sentence length and node-level supervision. Best: node-supervised Tree-LSTM at 48.4%.
